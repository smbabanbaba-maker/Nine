✳ INTRODUCING NATUREWORKS

For the environment.
For every person.

NatureWorks Global Solutions Ltd. is a sustainability-focused organization based in Kano, Nigeria.

We bring environmental care and community development together. Our work spans waste management, plastic recycling, climate action and sustainable agriculture, with a strong commitment to training, women's empowerment and the inclusion of persons with disabilities.

We believe that protecting the planet should also create practical skills, healthier communities and opportunities for people to build better livelihoods.
