✳ WHO WE ARE

Nature at heart.
People at the centre.

NatureWorks Global Solutions Ltd. is a sustainability-focused organization based in Kano, Nigeria.

We champion environmental stewardship and practical, community-driven solutions spanning waste management, plastic recycling, climate action and sustainable agriculture. Equally important, we invest in people — especially women, young people and persons with disabilities — through accessible learning and economic opportunity.

Where is NatureWorks based? +

NatureWorks Global Solutions Ltd. is based in Kano, Nigeria, and focuses on community-driven sustainability initiatives.

What areas does the organization work in? +

Environmental protection, waste management, plastic recycling, climate action, sustainable agriculture, and inclusive community development.

Who can participate in training programmes? +

NatureWorks prioritizes inclusive opportunities, including pathways for women, youth and persons with disabilities. Programme-specific requirements and availability will be shared when confirmed.

Can businesses and NGOs collaborate? +

Yes. The organization welcomes partnership conversations with businesses, development organizations, donors, investors and community groups.
